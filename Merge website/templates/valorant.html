Core Tactical Fundamentals

🎯 Crosshair Placement & Pre-aiming ▼
Keep your crosshair at head height at all times. Anticipate where enemy heads will appear when peeking angles to minimize reaction time.
🏃 Counter-strafing & Movement ▼
Tap the opposite movement key (e.g. D when moving left with A) to instantly come to a complete stop for 100% weapon accuracy before firing.
👁️ Peeking & Jiggle Peeking ▼
Use fast jiggle peeks to bait out enemy operator shots and gather information safely before committing to a wide swing.
💰 Economy & Utility Usage ▼
Coordinate eco buys, half buys, and full buys with your team. Never buy alone while the rest of your team saves.
